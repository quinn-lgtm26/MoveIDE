# Charter principles

The Charter is versioned and published as the value behind CoachPacket `charterVersion`. Protocol metrics cite principles by ID. Each principle and its video proxy must cite a peer-reviewed source before Quinn approves the Charter version.

| ID | Principle | Video proxy |
|---|---|---|
| P-BASE | The center of mass stays over the base of support for stable force production | Horizontal offset between the estimated center of mass and the midpoint of the foot or hand contacts, relative to a segment length |
| P-SEQ | Ballistic skills transfer energy outward from the core, peaking in the order hip, trunk, shoulder, elbow, wrist | Timestamp of each segment's peak angular speed; time gap and order between successive peaks |
| P-SSC | A countermovement (dip and drive) stores and releases elastic energy | Depth of the dip, how long the turnaround takes, and when the switch from lowering to driving happens (center of mass vertical speed reaches zero) |
| P-ROM | Joints move through the range the skill requires | Peak and minimum joint angles in each phase |
| P-ALIGN | Posture keeps load-bearing segments aligned | Each body segment's angle to vertical; alignment between segments (for example, deviation from a straight shoulder-hip-ankle line) |
| P-CONS | A skilled movement repeats consistently | Variation from rep to rep of any metric, across complete reps |

## Honesty rules

- Report proxies, never forces.
- An unavailable value is a valid outcome. Never fill it in by interpolation in the report.
- Every observation cites the measurement or frame IDs it rests on.
- A deviation that the body profile explains (for example, long femurs causing more trunk lean in a squat) is described as expected for those proportions, not flagged as a fault.
- Compare against the person's own baseline before population norms.
